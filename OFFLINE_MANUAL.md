# ✈️ OFFLINE MANUAL (NO NETWORK / AIRPLANE MODE)

This app runs **100% OFFLINE** on your phone. No Wi-Fi, no mobile data, and no internet are required.

---

## 🚀 3 Steps to Start Offline

### 1. Open Termux
Type this command and press Enter:
```bash
cube
```
*(Or: `bash ~/rubiks/start.sh`)*

---

### 2. Turn ON Bluetooth
- Put phone in **Airplane Mode** (Bluetooth can remain **ON**).
- Enable **Bluetooth** in your Android quick settings.

---

### 3. Open Chrome or Home Screen App
Go to:
👉 **`http://localhost:8080`**

Tap **"Connect Cube"**, select your GAN smart cube, and start solving!

---

## 🎯 How to Use:
1. **Scramble**: Follow the scramble sequence on your cube.
   - Moves auto-highlight in real time.
   - Wrong turns show amber correction sequence.
   - Finished scramble triggers chime & green **READY** signal.
2. **Solve**: Timer auto-starts on your first move and auto-stops when solved.
3. **Next Solve**: Fresh WCA scramble updates automatically.

---

## 💡 Quick Tips:
- **Set Solved / Recalibrate**: Tap **🎯 Set Solved** (or press key `R`).
- **New Scramble**: Tap **🔄 New** (or press key `N`).
- **Tap Timer**: Tap the big numbers to manually start/stop anytime.
